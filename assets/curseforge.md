# Bless the Blizzard

## Summary

Channel Blizzard and Toto's "Africa" plays. Stop channeling and the music stops. Your blizzards are blessed.

## Description

**Every Blizzard deserves a soundtrack.**

Bless the Blizzard is a tiny addon for mages. Whenever you channel Blizzard, it plays "Africa" through the game's music channel. When the channel ends (finished, interrupted or cancelled), the music stops.

When you log in as a mage who knows Blizzard, it lets you know: `YOUR BLIZZARDS ARE BLESSED`.

## Features
- Every rank of Blizzard counts.
- The music stops the moment the channel ends, and the addon only stops music it started, so it leaves your other music alone.
- `/btb test` plays the track for a few seconds so you can check your music volume.
- On characters that aren't mages it stays completely idle.
- No options, no saved variables, nothing to set up.

## Notes
- Works in WoW: Forever and Mists of Pandaria Classic.
- The track plays through the in-game music channel: turn music on and the music volume up to hear it.
- Only you hear it.
